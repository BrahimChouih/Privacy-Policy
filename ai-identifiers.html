By installing or using Our applications, You agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We may collect different types of information when You use Our applications:

1. Personal Data

2. Usage Data

Collected automatically when using Our apps, which may include:

  • Device type and operating system

3. Permissions

Certain apps may request access to device features (such as camera or storage) strictly to enable specific functionalities. These permissions are only used for their intended purpose.

How We Use Information

We use collected information for purposes such as:

  • Operating, improving, and maintaining Our applications
  • Monitoring usage patterns and performance to enhance user experience
  • Debugging, crash reporting, and security monitoring

  • Developing new features and improving existing services
  • Ensuring compliance with laws, policies, and security standards

Third-Party Services

Some of Our applications may integrate third-party services, such as:

  • Analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase) to understand usage trends

  • Crash reporting tools to diagnose technical issues

These third parties may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies for more details.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent Your personal data.
Information may only be shared in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers that support app functionality, analytics, or diagnostics

Data Retention

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary to provide Our services, fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, or comply with legal obligations.

Advertising and Analytics

  • Some of Our apps may display ads. Ads may be personalized based on general device information or usage data.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising through their device settings (e.g., resetting the advertising identifier or disabling ad personalization).

Children’s Privacy

Our applications are not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that information from a child under 13 has been collected, it will be deleted.

User Rights and Choices

Depending on Your jurisdiction, You may have rights such as:

  • Accessing, correcting, or deleting data that We hold

  • Limiting or withdrawing permissions previously granted

Most controls are available within device settings or the app itself.

Security

We take reasonable measures to safeguard collected data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and We cannot guarantee absolute security.

AI-Powered Image Analysis

Some features of our apps use Google Gemini, an artificial intelligence (AI) service provided by Google, to analyze images submitted by users and generate AI-powered identification or informational results.

Information We Send

When you use an AI analysis feature, we may securely transmit the following information to Google Gemini:

  • The photo you select or capture for analysis.
  • The analysis option (AI context) you select within the app.

We do not intentionally send your name, email address, contacts, or other personal information for AI analysis unless you voluntarily provide such information as part of the submitted content.

Purpose of Processing

The information is transmitted solely to generate the AI-powered result you requested and to provide the functionality of the AI analysis feature.

Third-Party AI Provider

AI analysis is performed using Google Gemini, an AI service provided by Google. The submitted information is processed only to generate the requested AI response. Google's handling of this information is governed by its own privacy practices. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy and applicable Google Gemini documentation for more information about how Google processes data.

Data Security

Information sent for AI analysis is transmitted using secure encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We take reasonable measures to protect information during transmission.

AI Limitations

AI-generated results are provided for informational purposes only. While we strive to provide useful and accurate results, AI-generated content may contain inaccuracies or omissions and should not be considered professional, medical, legal, financial, or other expert advice. You should use your own judgment and consult a qualified professional when appropriate.
